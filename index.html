Common Questions

Good questions deserve straight answers.

Does Arlo read all of my emails?

Arlo scans your Gmail inbox to find emails that look like they need action — requests, follow-ups, bills, or deadlines. Newsletters, receipts, and automated notifications go to the Updates shelf or are ignored entirely. Arlo never stores your email content on its servers beyond what's needed to show you the task.

Does Arlo work without Gmail?

Yes. You can capture tasks by voice, type them directly, or send anything to your personal Arlo email address. Gmail and Calendar are optional integrations — Arlo is fully usable without connecting either.

What happens to emails that aren't tasks?

They stay in Gmail, untouched. Arlo only surfaces the ones that need you to do something. Everything else — confirmations, marketing, notifications — either goes to the Updates shelf or doesn't appear in Arlo at all.

Do I need to organize anything manually?

No. Arlo assigns every item to Now, Next, or Later automatically. You can move things around if you want, but you never have to. Most people open the app and just start with whatever's in Now.

Can I connect more than one Gmail account?

Yes. You can connect multiple Gmail accounts and Arlo pulls tasks from all of them into one unified list. Everything stays sorted the same way regardless of which account it came from.

Does Arlo work with Calendly, Zoom, or Google Meet?

Yes — through any calendar synced to your iPhone. When someone books time via Calendly, or you accept a Zoom or Google Meet invite, it lands in your calendar. Arlo reads it from there and shows it on your Home alongside your tasks. No separate setup needed for any scheduling tool.

What's the difference between free and Premium?

The core system — capture, sorting, Gmail sync, voice notes, forwarding — is free. Premium adds the AI layer: morning briefings, Focus Mode, Catch Up, Ask Arlo, and personalized insights that learn your habits over time.
